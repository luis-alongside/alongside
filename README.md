# Alongside

A free, open-source companion for parents supporting a child or teenager through anorexia nervosa recovery, especially during family-based treatment (FBT) at home.

It works as an app on your phone's home screen. There's no app store, no account and no cost, and everything you enter stays on your phone.

## What it does

- **Today:** a meal and snack checklist, a daily tip, and alerts.
- **Menus and recipes:** a built-in planner that uses your child's liked foods, brings back feared foods gently (one a day at most), respects allergies and family diet, and follows typical UK eating-disorder service portions. An optional "Ask Claude" button hands a ready-made request to your own Claude app.
- **Meal diary:** record how much was eaten, time taken, distress level, behaviours noticed, and notes. You can type the notes or speak them.
- **Patterns:** works out on the phone which meals are hardest, when distress peaks, whether things are improving, and suggests ideas and questions for your team. It never suggests less food.
- **Health checks:** pulse, blood pressure, weight, height and temperature, with charts and a pulse alert set by your team.
- **Guide:** practical, evidence-based advice for mealtimes, behaviours, food and looking after yourself.
- **Research updates:** short, parent-friendly summaries of new trusted research and guidance (from `updates.json`).
- **Urgent help:** UK emergency guidance and helplines.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the app's web address in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Alongside from your home screen. It works offline.

## Your data

All data is kept in the phone's browser storage for this app. Use **Profile → Save a backup** regularly. Nothing is sent to any server.

## Important

Alongside shares general guidance for families. It does not replace your child's treatment team. Always follow their advice.

## Files

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`: the app
- `meals.js`: the meal library and menu planner
- `updates.json`: research updates (refreshed by a daily job)
- `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`: what lets it install and work offline
